🤟 About HandTalk

HandTalk is a sign language communication platform designed to reduce communication barriers between sign language users and people who may not understand sign language.

📷 Camera Detection

Uses camera-based hand tracking to detect hand movements.

📝 Text Translation

Converts recognized gestures into readable English text.

🔊 Voice Output

Converts translated text into speech.

📚 Learning

Helps beginners learn commonly used signs.
